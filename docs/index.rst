====================
DeePTB Documentation
====================

DeePTB builds E3-equivariant neural models for LCAO Hamiltonians and related
quantum operators.  The ``0726-light`` line focuses on the maintained E3,
LEM/EMol embedding, flow, block-native, LMDB, and training routes.
